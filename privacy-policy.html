Ddott.TV ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Ddott.TV video platform, including our website (ddott.live), mobile applications, and related services (collectively, the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

By using Ddott.TV, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

  • Name, email address, username, and profile details you provide when creating an account
  • Profile photo and other content you choose to add to your profile
  • Authentication data used to sign you in securely

Content You Create

  • Videos and Drotts (short-form videos) you upload
  • Comments, likes, playlists, and other interactions with content
  • Messages sent through Community and Music Room features
  • Content you post in Communities and Collabs

Usage & Technical Data

  • Device information, IP address, and browser type
  • Viewing history, watch time, and interaction data used to power recommendations
  • Log data collected automatically when you use the Service

Payment & Wallet Data

  • EMO Coin purchase and transaction history
  • Cashout requests and wallet activity
  • Payment confirmation data from our payment processor (we do not store your full card or bank details)

Advertiser Data

  • If you use Ad Studio, we collect information related to ad campaigns, ad interactions, and pricing tier selections

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, operate, and maintain the Service
  • To personalize your feed, recommendations, and search results
  • To process EMO Coin purchases, rewards, and cashouts
  • To enable social features such as following, messaging, and community interaction
  • To detect, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, and security issues
  • To respond to support requests, bug reports, and complaints
  • To communicate with you about updates, policies, or account activity
  • To comply with legal obligations

3. Payments & EMO Coins

EMO Coin purchases are processed securely through Razorpay, our third-party payment processor. When you make a purchase, Razorpay handles your payment details directly — Ddott.TV does not store your full card, UPI, or bank account information.

To comply with app store policies, coin purchases made through our mobile app may redirect you to our website (ddott.live) to complete payment.

We retain records of your EMO Coin balance, transaction history, and cashout requests as needed to operate the platform and for accounting and fraud-prevention purposes.

4. Third-Party Services

We work with the following categories of service providers to operate Ddott.TV:

Supabase — database & authentication Cloudflare — hosting, CDN & workers Razorpay — payment processing

These providers process data on our behalf and are contractually obligated to protect your information. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

5. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored using industry-standard security practices, including row-level security on our database and encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) for all data in transit. While we work hard to protect your information, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

6. Your Rights & Choices

  • You can access and update your profile information at any time within the app
  • You can request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us
  • You can control notification preferences within the app settings

7. Children's Privacy

Ddott.TV is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

8. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service. If you request account deletion, we will delete or anonymize your personal data, except where retention is required for legal, accounting, or fraud-prevention purposes.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by posting the updated policy on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
