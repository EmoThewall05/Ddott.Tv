Safety System

Emowall SOS & Child Protection

Safety is not a feature. It is the foundation. Emowall AI 2.0 powers a real-time emergency protocol integrated directly into Ddott TV's network.

🛡

SOS Live Streaming Gate

When an SOS trigger activates on a connected device, the system requires encrypted app-approval via the Emowall companion app. After parental biometric or PIN clearance, an encrypted live stream routes directly to Ddott.TV's network.

LIVE PROTOCOL
🔐

Encrypted Approval Chain

Multi-layer encryption at every step. Parental biometric or PIN clearance required before any stream goes live. No bypass. No backdoors. Verified by design.

  • Device-level SOS trigger detection
  • Companion app encrypted handshake
  • Biometric / PIN parental clearance
  • Encrypted stream routing to Ddott TV
🤖

Background AI Shield

Continuous AI monitoring filters malicious inputs, bot commands, and injection attempts before they reach the network. Exact location data remains confidential — accessible to legal authorities only.

  • Real-time malicious input filtering
  • Bot command detection & blocking
  • Injection attempt prevention
  • Confidential location encryption
🌐

Decentralized Crowd Support

Community-powered rescue coordination. When SOS is active, decentralized crowd-support aids real-time rescue efforts without exposing sensitive victim data. The network helps, anonymously.

  • Anonymous crowd-support routing
  • Rescue coordination aid
  • Zero data exposure to public
  • Legal authority integration ready
